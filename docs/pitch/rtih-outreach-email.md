The problem we are solving
Most mid-sized Indian hospitals still run a single patient episode across
disconnected systems — paper files for records, one software for the front desk,
Excel trackers for insurance claims, and informal WhatsApp messages for
coordination. The result is clinician time lost to documentation, delayed and
incomplete discharge summaries, claim denials and revenue leakage, and weak
readiness for ABDM, HMIS and NABH compliance.

What we have built
Aumrti brings the full patient journey — registration, OPD, IPD, nursing, lab,
radiology, pharmacy, billing, MRD, discharge and insurance/TPA — onto one
workflow spine, with AI embedded in the workflow rather than bolted on:

  • Multilingual AI Scribe that converts a doctor's spoken consultation into a
    structured clinical note and orders, built on the Government of India's
    Bhashini language stack for Indian languages.
  • Episode-aware AI discharge summaries, ICD coding assistance and automated
    revenue-leak detection across the billing cycle.
  • India-specific compliance built in — ABDM/ABHA linking and FHIR record
    sharing, PMJAY and CGHS eligibility workflows, HMIS reporting and NABH
    quality modules.
  • Designed for real hospital conditions — offline-first sync for unreliable
    connectivity, and structured, auditable WhatsApp communication.

Current stage
Aumrti is a working prototype with functional depth across more than 55 hospital
modules, and is registered under MSME (Udyam No. [Your Udyam Number]). We have not
yet run a live hospital deployment — validating the product inside real hospital
operations is precisely the next step, and the reason we are approaching RTIH.

What we are seeking from RTIH, Amaravati
  1. Incubation support and mentorship through your programme / Centre of
     Excellence.
  2. Design-partner or pilot access to a government or partner hospital in Andhra
     Pradesh, to validate clinician time saved, discharge turnaround and claim
     readiness with live data.
  3. Consideration for seed/grant support to harden the platform for pilot
     deployment over the next 6–12 months.

Andhra Pradesh's push on digital health infrastructure and its Amaravati
innovation ecosystem make it the right place to prove this out, and a successful
pilot here would be directly replicable across district and mid-sized hospitals
in the state.

I have attached our pitch deck for your review. I am available at your
