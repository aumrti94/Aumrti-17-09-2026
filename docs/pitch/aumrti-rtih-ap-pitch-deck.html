Underlying innovation
Workflow-native AI plus India-ready infrastructure

Our innovation is not a single AI feature. It is the orchestration layer around it.

Workflow-native AI

Multilingual voice capture becomes structured clinical data instead of free-text only. AI outputs are designed to feed notes, orders, discharge summaries and claim narratives.

India-specific hospital stack

Aumrti is being built around ABDM, HMIS, MRD, payer and TPA workflows, and communication patterns already used in Indian hospitals, including structured WhatsApp.

Trust, safety and deployability

Human review, audit logging, tenancy isolation, PHI-aware data handling and offline synchronization help the product work in high-trust, low-connectivity environments.
